About SILVIET

Building Industrial Capacity for the Future

A strategic partner committed to advancing precipitated silica solutions in Southeast Asia through quality, innovation, and long-term collaboration.

Our Vision

To become the trusted strategic partner for premium precipitated silica in Southeast Asia, contributing to sustainable industrial development across the region.

Our Mission

To provide consistent, high-quality silicon dioxide solutions while building local industrial capacity and fostering long-term partnerships with manufacturers worldwide.

Our Story

From strategic vision to industrial reality

SILVIET was founded with a clear purpose: to address the growing demand for quality precipitated silica in Southeast Asia while building sustainable local industrial capacity.

Vietnam's strategic location, robust logistics infrastructure, and access to raw materials make it an ideal hub for silica production and distribution. We recognized this opportunity and committed to a methodical, phased approach to development.

Our approach prioritizes long-term value over short-term gains. We believe in building genuine partnerships with our clients, understanding their needs, and growing together as the industry evolves.

Development Roadmap

A phased approach to sustainable growth

Phase 1: Foundation (0-18 months)

Commercial & Logistics Development

Establishing commercial operations, building logistics networks, developing client relationships, and accumulating market data. Focus on becoming a reliable trading and sourcing partner.

Phase 2: Development (18-36 months)

Pilot Production & Technology

Initiating pilot production, testing product formulations, developing technical expertise, and establishing quality control systems. Building partnerships with technology providers.

Phase 3 — Production Expansion (Years 3–6)

Enhancing Capacity and Product Diversification

Develop silica production capacity in Vietnam, reduce dependence on imports, expand product portfolio, and serve broader industrial applications.

Phase 4 — Regional Industrial Platform

Integrated Chemical and Materials Supply Network

Expand production capacity, scale trading & logistics, build a comprehensive supply platform, and serve domestic & global markets.
