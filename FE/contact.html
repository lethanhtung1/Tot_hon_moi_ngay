Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common inquiries

What industries do you serve?

SilViet supports: Food, Feed, Tire manufacturing, Rubber products, Coatings & paints, Agricultural chemicals, and Specialty materials.

What products do you supply?

We supply Precipitated silica, other industrial materials, and custom sourcing solutions based on client needs.

What is your typical order quantity?

Our supply is flexible: from Pallet (for testing) to Full container (for bulk industrial supply).
